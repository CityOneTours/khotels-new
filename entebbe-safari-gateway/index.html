Your First and Last Night in Uganda

K Hotels is a convenient first or last night in Uganda, with 48 rooms on Hill Road in Entebbe and the airport 12 to 14 minutes away by car.

Start your Uganda safari rested

Arrive, check in from 2:00 pm, and settle in before travelling onward. Our team can help coordinate an early departure, and packed breakfast can be discussed when you book.

Practical support for your journey

Ask our front office about luggage storage, early check-out and transport arrangements. Airport pick-ups are available by paid shuttle and must be pre-booked. Direct bookings include a complimentary one-way airport drop-off.

For safari routes, departure points and operator arrangements, please confirm details with your chosen safari company before travel.

Plan an airport stay

Experience luxury, comfort, and hospitality redefined. K Hotels offers elegant spaces, world-class amenities, and exceptional service to make every stay memorable.
