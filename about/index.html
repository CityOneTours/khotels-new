About K Hotels Entebbe

Experiencing K Hotels

Step into a world of relaxation and opulence at K Hotels, a fully furnished 4 star luxury hotel in the city of Entebbe town, offering elegance and comfort in a soothing atmosphere, with stunning views of the great Lake Victoria.

K Hotels is a well-known boutique hotel with convenient access to all parts of the city. Guests can enjoy social amenities such as shopping centres, the Botanical Gardens, the Wildlife Education Centre, Lake Victoria beaches, international diplomatic neighbourhoods, international schools, and it is just 10 minutes away from the airport.

The ambience of K Hotels is casual and friendly. Personalized service is at the heart of our values. Guests can mingle in the Lounge and VIP areas for residents or relax in the terrace swimming pool with unobstructed views of Lake Victoria. K Hotels combines gracious hospitality with contemporary style.

K Hotels is well known Boutique hotel in Entebbe town, within close proximity to the Central Business District, with convenient access to all parts of the city, and essential social amenities like shopping centres, prime residential areas, international diplomatic neighbourhoods, and international schools in the heart of Entebbe city.

Looking for the perfect space for your meeting, special event or wedding? K Hotels has all you could possibly need in a modern hotel, situated in a wonderful location. With easy access to Kampala, connecting you to all major routes leading to Uganda’s natural, special and diverse beauty that inspired Winston Churchill to describe Uganda as “The Pearl of Africa”.

K Hotels is known to have East Africa’s most prestigious and well-furnished modern hotel with 48 luxury rooms and exceptional amenities. Enhance your stay with personalised services for a memorable, comfortable, long or short-term stay in UGANDA.
