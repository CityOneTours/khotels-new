Conference & Meeting Venue in Entebbe

K Hotels has four conference halls in Entebbe, 12 to 14 minutes from Entebbe International Airport. Victoria Hall seats up to 185 guests theatre-style, and three smaller halls suit board meetings, workshops and breakout sessions. Every event comes with audio-visual equipment, high-speed Wi-Fi, catering and planning support from our team.

Easy for delegates to reach

We are 12 to 14 minutes from Entebbe International Airport. Our airport shuttle can be pre-booked for arriving delegates, and guests who book their rooms direct receive a free one-way drop-off to the airport.

Stay where you meet

48 rooms and suites on site, from USD 105 a night with breakfast, so delegates can stay where they meet. Day-use rooms from USD 75 suit speakers and visitors who fly in for the day.

Food and evenings

Catering comes from Kurries, our all-day restaurant serving Continental and Indian cuisine. For receptions and networking, K Rooftop is open from 4:00 pm to 1:00 am.

Service you can rely on

Rated 4.8 out of 5 from 6,263 Google reviews and #1 of 65 hotels in Entebbe on Tripadvisor, with event planning support from enquiry to wrap-up.

Plan your event

Tell us your date, number of guests and preferred layout using the enquiry form, call +256 777 846 000 or email info@khotels.ug, and our team will send a proposal.

Conference FAQs

What is the largest conference hall at K Hotels?

Victoria Hall seats up to 185 guests theatre-style, 165 classroom-style or 120 in a boardroom layout.

Do you have rooms for small meetings?

Yes. Sunset Hall and Equator Hall seat 12 to 20 guests, and Sunrise Hall seats up to 60 theatre-style.

Can delegates stay at the hotel?

Yes. K Hotels has 48 rooms and suites, from USD 105 a night with breakfast included.

How far is the venue from Entebbe Airport?

12 to 14 minutes by car. Airport shuttles can be pre-booked for a fee.
