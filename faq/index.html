Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about staying, dining and meeting at K Hotels Entebbe.

How far is K Hotels from Entebbe Airport?

About 12 to 14 minutes by car.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Guests who book directly with K Hotels receive a complimentary one-way drop-off to the airport. Airport pick-ups are available on our paid shuttle and must be booked in advance.

Can I book a room for a few hours before my flight?

Yes. Day-use rooms start at USD 75.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon.

What is the largest conference hall at K Hotels?

Victoria Hall seats up to 185 guests theatre-style, 165 classroom-style or 120 in a boardroom layout.

Do you have rooms for small meetings?

Yes. Sunset Hall and Equator Hall seat 12 to 20 guests, and Sunrise Hall seats up to 60 theatre-style.

Can delegates stay at the hotel?

Yes. K Hotels has 48 rooms and suites, from USD 105 a night with breakfast included.

How far is the venue from Entebbe Airport?

12 to 14 minutes by car. Airport shuttles can be pre-booked for a fee.

What time does check-in start?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm.

When is check-out?

Check-out is by 12:00 noon.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are not allowed at K Hotels.

How many rooms does K Hotels have?

K Hotels has 48 rooms and suites.

What is the DIRECT26 offer?

Use code DIRECT26 for 20% off when booking direct.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast and Wi-Fi are included in the published room rates.

Does the hotel have a pool?

Yes. K Hotels has an outdoor swimming pool.

Are pool day passes available?

Day passes for the pool and health club are available. Please contact the hotel for current details.

Is there a paid airport shuttle?

Yes. Airport shuttles are available for a fee and must be pre-booked.

Is the front office open 24 hours?

Yes. The front office is available around the clock at info@khotels.ug.

Does K Hotels have a restaurant?

Kurries serves Continental and Indian cuisine throughout the day.

What are K Rooftop hours?

K Rooftop is open from 4:00 pm to 1:00 am.

Is Wi-Fi available?

High-speed Wi-Fi is included for hotel guests.

What room types are available?

Classic Room, Classic Twin Room, Classic Suite, Classic Lake Suite and Junior Lake Suite.

How many people can Victoria Hall host?

Victoria Hall seats up to 185 guests theatre-style, 165 classroom-style or 120 in a boardroom layout.
