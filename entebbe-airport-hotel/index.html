Your Entebbe Airport Hotel

K Hotels is a 4-star hotel on Hill Road, Entebbe, 12 to 14 minutes by car from Entebbe International Airport. Book direct and your one-way drop-off to the airport is included. For layovers and late flights, day-use rooms start at USD 75.

Arriving in Uganda

Check-in is from 2:00 pm, and our front office team is available around the clock at fo.ebb@khotels.ug. Need a ride from the airport? Our airport shuttle is available for a fee and must be booked in advance; share your flight number and arrival time when you book.

Flying out

Check-out is by 12:00 noon. Guests who book direct receive a complimentary one-way drop-off to Entebbe International Airport. Kurries is open throughout the day, so you can eat before an early or late flight.

Layovers and late flights

Day-use rooms start at USD 75, so you can rest and freshen up between flights. Day passes are also available for our pool and health club.

Why travellers choose K Hotels

Rated 4.8 out of 5 from 6,263 Google reviews, #1 of 65 hotels in Entebbe on Tripadvisor every year since we opened, and named among Tripadvisor's Top 25 Hotels in Africa in 2022 and 2023.

Airport stay FAQs

How far is K Hotels from Entebbe Airport?

About 12 to 14 minutes by car.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Guests who book directly with K Hotels receive a complimentary one-way drop-off to the airport. Airport pick-ups are available on our paid shuttle and must be booked in advance.

Can I book a room for a few hours before my flight?

Yes. Day-use rooms start at USD 75.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out is by 12:00 noon.
